Greek in, glyphs out

Type a message in Greek. Every letter becomes a glowing glyph taken from the grim video, and the whole message downloads as an animated GIF.

Accents and capitals are ignored. Numbers and punctuation pass through unchanged.

The key

Glyphs are assigned in the order they appear in the video, α first. Select a glyph to add its letter to your message.

The video shows 21 different glyphs. To cover all 24 Greek letters, three of them (the hook, the squiggle and the arrow) also appear mirrored. The final sigma ς uses the same glyph as σ.
